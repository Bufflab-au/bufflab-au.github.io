Common Questions

How much does ceramic coating cost in Brisbane?

It depends on your vehicle's size, paint condition and which coating you choose. We offer three graphene coatings at different price points, and every coating includes full paint decontamination, machine polish prep and a written warranty. Text us your car and suburb for a free, exact quote.

How much is a cut and polish?

Every cut & polish includes a full exterior wash and clay, contamination removal, machine polishing and a paint sealant finish. Price depends on your car's size and paint condition — text us a few photos for a free quote.

What can a machine buff fix on my car?

A machine cut & polish (a professional car buff) removes swirl marks, oxidation, light scratches and dull paint, restoring the paint's true depth and gloss. Every job includes a full exterior wash and clay, contamination removal and a paint sealant finish. Text us a few photos of your paint and we'll let you know what can be corrected.

Which coating brands do you use?

We use Fireball Butterfly Graphene, Fireball FX88 Graphene and Phoenix E.O.D Graphene Coating. We're also a certified SkyGloss installer offering SkyGloss FUSION, RESIN and SHIELD. Each sits at a different price point, so we'll recommend the right one for your car and budget.

What's the difference between a cut & polish and ceramic coating?

A cut & polish (paint correction) removes swirl marks, oxidation and light scratches to restore gloss. Ceramic coating is a protective layer that bonds to the paint to keep it looking that way for years. For the best result, most cars get a polish first, then a coating.

Do your ceramic coatings come with a warranty?

Yes. Every ceramic coating we apply comes with a written warranty. Each coating also includes full paint decontamination and machine polish prep, so it bonds properly to clean, corrected paint. Text us your car and suburb and we'll go through the options with your quote.

How long does a graphene or ceramic coating last?

Years, not weeks. Our professional-grade graphene coatings bond to your paint and stay hydrophobic and UV protected, guarding against paint fade. Each comes with a written warranty, and we'll explain what to expect from each of our three coatings when we quote. Text us your car and suburb for a free quote.
